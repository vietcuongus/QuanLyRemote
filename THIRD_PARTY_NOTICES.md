# Third-party components

Exact versions and transitive dependencies are pinned in package-lock.json.
Dependency copyright/license files are retained in the desktop distribution.
Electron distributes Chromium/Node notices in LICENSES.chromium.html.

| Component | License | Upstream |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Electron | MIT; bundled Chromium/Node have their own notices | https://github.com/electron/electron |
| React / React DOM | MIT | https://github.com/facebook/react |
| xterm.js and addons | MIT | https://github.com/xtermjs/xterm.js |
| ssh2 | MIT | https://github.com/mscdex/ssh2 |
| Lucide icons | ISC | https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide |

Build/test tools: Vite, electron-builder, Playwright and their dependencies.
Consult their installed LICENSE files for notices. UniTerm, Tabby and Electerm are
research references; their source code, logos and screenshots are not distributed.
